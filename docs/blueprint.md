# **App Name**: ChirpID

## Core Features:

- Live Audio Recording: Record audio directly through the app for immediate analysis.
- Audio Upload: Upload pre-recorded audio files from the user's device.
- Bird Identification: Use an AI tool to analyze audio and identify bird species.
- Species Details Display: Display detailed information about identified bird species, including images and descriptions.
- Personal Bird Log: Log identified birds to a personal list for tracking and reference.
- Bird Prediction: Use an AI tool to predict what birds may be in an area based on previous sightings in the area. Use scientific studies, bird guide books, citizen science results and previous recordings from our app
- Birds Specific Audio Analysis: Take audio recordings and separate out different bird songs into separate audio files and then identify the birds of each file.
- Environment Profile: Make up a profile of the environment the user is in based on location data. The profile should include information such as if it is a natural environment or man made, what sort of vegetation is in the area, what is the weather like, is the area tropical or temperate, have there been bushfires or flooding. If the user does not want to share location or location is unavailble provided basics options for them to select
- Likelihood Ranking: Compare the environmental profile you have created based on user location or their input to research data and public information on bird habitats, environment preferences, migration patterns. Generate a list of birds that would possibly be found in this area. Rate how likely you are to see them based on how well the location compares to available data.
- Top Bird Suggestions: Combine information from bird prediction, bird specific audio analysis and likelihood ranking and decide which top three birds the recorded audio is most likely to be. The AI will use a tool to consider environmental factors, audio analysis, and sighting data.
- Bird Suggestion Display: Display an image of the top bird suggestion to the user. Give them a chance to choose this bird or view the other 2 options in case you got the bird wrong as bird songs can be hard to identify.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Black (#000000) for a clean, modern look.
- Background color: White (#FFFFFF), providing high contrast.
- Accent color: Light gray (#D3D3D3), offering subtle highlights and visual separation.
- Font pairing: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for both headlines and body text.
- Use simple, monochrome icons representing different bird species and app functions.
- Simple and intuitive layout, emphasizing ease of use with a minimalist design.
